# Bridge Buddies — PRD

## One-liner
An iOS app that pairs CMU students — especially domestic and international students — for low-pressure, in-person connection based on shared interests, comfort levels, and social pace.

## Problem
Students are surrounded by thousands of people but struggle to find someone they're actually comfortable spending time with. International students face this compounded by unfamiliar culture and social structure, with no easy way to meet domestic students outside class. Existing social apps optimize for volume of matches, not comfort or follow-through into real-world interaction.

## Target user
Central Michigan University students, specifically ones navigating a new or unfamiliar social environment — international students settling in, domestic students who want to be more social, and anyone who's introverted, homesick, or new to campus and wants a structured, low-pressure way to meet people.

## MVP scope (must-have)
- CMU-email verified auth (Apple Sign-In + email/password)
- School selection flow (CMU-only for v1, architecture supports future multi-school)
- Profile creation/editing
- Identity/comfort bubble selection (predefined + custom)
- Matching (receive/accept/decline based on interests, languages, schedule, comfort bubbles)
- Chat (list + thread — thread UI spec pending, see Rules.md)
- Campus Resources (searchable directory, opens external webpages, MapKit for location-based resources)

## Explicitly out of scope (for now)
- Buddy Challenges / mini challenges
- Campus Pulse feed
- Campus/Meet & Mingle events
- Communities
- Multi-school rollout (MSU, U-M) — architecture allows it, not built
- Dark mode

## Success looks like
Daily active matches and chats happening between real CMU students — not just a working demo, but actual usage.
